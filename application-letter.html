Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my interest in an industrial attachment opportunity in Data Analytics within your organisation. I am eager to complement my academic studies with practical workplace exposure and develop a deeper understanding of how data supports business operations and informed decision-making.

Through my studies and independent learning, I am developing foundational skills in Microsoft Excel and Structured Query Language (SQL). I am interested in learning how these tools are used to organise information, retrieve data, identify patterns and support analytical reporting. I am prepared to approach assigned responsibilities with professionalism, curiosity and a commitment to improving my capabilities. I would also value constructive feedback that helps me connect academic knowledge with practical business needs.

I would appreciate the opportunity to be considered for an attachment placement in Data Analytics. My curriculum vitae is available to provide further information about my educational background, skills and interests.
